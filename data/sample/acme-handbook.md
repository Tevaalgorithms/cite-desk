# Acme Robotics Employee Handbook

## About this handbook

This handbook describes how Acme Robotics employees work. It is updated each quarter by the People team. Contractors are covered only where a section says so.

## Working hours

### Core hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time on weekdays. Employees choose their other hours with their manager.

### Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days a week with manager approval. Remote work needs a secure home network and a company laptop.

## Time off

### Vacation

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid vacation each year. Unused vacation days carry over for up to 5 days into the next year. Contractors do not receive paid vacation.

### Sick leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days each year. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick days.

### Parental leave

New parents receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. The leave can start up to 2 weeks before the expected birth or adoption date.

### Public holidays

Acme Robotics observes 9 public holidays each year. The People team publishes the list every December.

## Expenses

### Travel

Book flights and hotels through the company travel portal. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours.

### Meals

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 60 dollars per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

### Receipts

Submit expense reports within 30 days of the trip. Every expense over 25 dollars needs a receipt.

## Equipment

### Laptops

Every new employee receives a company laptop on their first day. Report a lost or stolen laptop to IT immediately.

### Returning equipment

When you leave the company you must return your laptop within 5 business days. Contact IT to arrange a courier.

## Security

### Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is required for every administrator account.

### Reporting incidents

Report a suspected security incident to the security team within 24 hours. Do not try to investigate it yourself.

## Leaving the company

### Offboarding

Your last day is set with your manager and the People team. Your access to company systems ends at the close of your last day.
