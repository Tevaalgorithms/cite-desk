# Acme Robotics IT Support Guide

## About this guide

This guide explains how employees get help from the IT team at Acme Robotics. It is written for the demo of CiteDesk and all details are fictional.

## Getting help

### Helpdesk hours

The IT helpdesk is open from 8:00 to 18:00 on weekdays. Outside these hours, urgent problems can be reported to the on-call engineer through the emergency line. The helpdesk is closed on public holidays.

### Opening a ticket

Open a support ticket through the IT portal and describe the problem in one or two sentences. Tickets marked urgent are answered within 1 hour. All other tickets are answered within 1 business day.

## Accounts

### Resetting a password

Employees can reset their own password through the self-service page. If the self-service page does not work, contact the helpdesk with a photo of your badge so your identity can be checked.

## Software

### Requesting software

Ask for new software by opening a ticket and naming the tool and the reason. The manager of the requester must approve every paid license. Free tools on the approved list are installed within 2 business days.

## Hardware

### Broken devices

Report a broken laptop, monitor or phone to the helpdesk on the same day. A loan laptop is available for up to 5 business days while the device is repaired.
