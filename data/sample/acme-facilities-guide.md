# Acme Robotics Office and Facilities Guide

## About this guide

This guide covers the Acme Robotics offices. It is written for employees and is updated by the Facilities team each year. All details are fictional.

## Office access

### Badges

Every employee receives an access badge on their first day. The badge opens the main entrance and the floor where the employee works. Report a lost badge to Facilities on the same day so it can be deactivated.

### Opening hours

The office is open from 7:00 to 19:00 on weekdays. It is closed on weekends and public holidays. Employees who need weekend access must ask Facilities for approval at least 2 days in advance.

## Visitors

### Registering a visitor

Every visitor must be registered at the front desk and wears a visitor badge at all times. Employees must register visitors at least 1 day before the visit. Visitors may not be left alone on a floor.

## Meeting rooms

### Booking a room

Book meeting rooms through the calendar system. A room booking is released automatically if nobody checks in within 10 minutes. Rooms can be booked up to 30 days ahead.

## Food and drink

### Cafeteria

The cafeteria serves lunch from 11:30 to 14:00. A lunch costs 8 dollars and can be paid by badge. Coffee and tea are free on every floor.

## Parking

### Parking permits

Parking permits cost 45 dollars per month and are deducted from salary. Employees apply for a permit through Facilities. Bicycle storage is free and does not need a permit.

## Dress code

### What to wear

The dress code is smart casual. Safety shoes are required in the workshop and the robot test lab. Visitors to the workshop receive safety glasses at the front desk.

## Wi-Fi

### Guest network

Visitors can use the guest Wi-Fi network named AcmeGuest. The guest password is shown on the screen at the front desk and changes every week. Employees must use the corporate network, not the guest network.
